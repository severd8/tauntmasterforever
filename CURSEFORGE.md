# TauntMaster Forever

**Click a party member's bar to taunt their target off them.**

TauntMaster Forever is a tanking addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. It shows a bar for every member of your party or raid, colors each bar by how much threat that player has, and lets you rescue them with one click: no targeting, no macros, no fumbling.

It's a from-scratch rebuild of the classic *TauntMaster* addon, written for Forever's modern addon rules.

## What it does

- **One click to taunt.** Click a player's bar and your taunt is cast on the enemy they're fighting. Your own target doesn't change.
- **See trouble early.** Bars change color as a player gains threat, and alerts tell you when someone pulls aggro or a mob turns away from you.
- **Your class's taunts, ready to go.** Warriors, Druids and Paladins are set up automatically, and every click, key or controller button can be bound to what you like.
- **Made to fit you.** Parties and raids, sizes, colors, alerts and announcements are all in one options window. Hover over any setting to see what it does.

## Getting started

Install it with the CurseForge app, or unzip the download into your WoW: Forever `Interface\AddOns` folder, then restart the game or type `/reload`.

Type `/tm` to open the options. Drag the red header to move the bars.

See the **Changelog** on the Files tab for what's new in each version, and the [full guide on GitHub](https://github.com/severd8/tauntmasterforever#readme) for every feature and command.

## Good to know

- **It never acts on its own.** Every taunt is your click.
- **A click taunts whatever that player has targeted.** If a healer has a friend targeted, there's nothing to taunt off them with a click.
- **Changes to the bars' size and position wait until combat ends.** That's a game rule for addons.

## All addons by me

- **TauntMaster Forever**: one-click taunts off your healers and DPS, for tanks.
- **ToppedOff Forever**: reminds you when buffs, food, reagents, ammo or gear need topping off.
- **Outfitter Forever**: save your gear as outfits and switch between them with one click, or let them change by themselves.
- **BattleText Forever**: scrolling combat text for your hits, heals and the damage you take.
- **AuctionHouse Forever**: a bigger Auction House window, so long item names stop getting cut off.
- **GatherPath Forever**: the shortest gathering route through your GatherMate2 herb and ore nodes, on the world map and minimap.
- **ARS Forever** (Azeroth Revenue Service): see how much gold a player has made and the most they've ever held, for you and anyone you inspect.
